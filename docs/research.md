Statuspage and Vantage were excluded because those families were built earlier tonight. This is not recruitment or applicant tracking.

## Annual bill

A [published UK government contract](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Notice/Attachment/6c39309c-eabf-4e92-9a73-c4aa8a197b2a) records GBP 112,560 for each of the first two subscription years, excluding VAT, starting September 2023. That proves a six-figure annual contract existed. It is historical public-sector evidence, not a current list price, an NZ/AU small-business invoice or a promise of savings. The document was available in indexed text this run; its direct download returned an access error. The amount is kept in selection evidence, not advertised as a vendor price.

[Orgvue's current commercial terms](https://www.orgvue.com/legal/terms-and-conditions/orgvue-subscription-agreement/) describe annual subscription invoicing and separately billed professional services. The site gives no numeric public subscription tariff. The comparison page therefore uses privately quoted pricing and no dollar figure. [Orgvue's contact page](https://www.orgvue.com/contact/) lists Sydney presence, which is not proof that an EDNA customer uses it.

These are editorial selection scores, not market measurements. Orgvue's buyer score is deliberately one: local customer records searched in this run did not establish usage or an invoice, and live CRM spend was not verified. The enterprise annual-bill evidence qualifies it under the charter's large-tool allowance, while the narrow local planning desk is the buildable subset.

[AG5 pricing](https://www.ag5.com/pricing/) is now quoted across Core, Accelerate and Enterprise. Its [cost article](https://www.ag5.com/skills-management-software-cost/) describes older lower-cost editions, insufficient to establish a qualifying five-figure current bill. [Kompyte's plans](https://www.kompyte.com/plans?view=Schedule) price by tracked competitors, users and features without a public amount. Its web monitoring is less database-shaped and a standard full export was not established. Both lost the annual-bill evidence test.

The search score reflects alternative-page competition, including [G2's Orgvue alternatives](https://www.g2.com/products/orgvue/competitors/alternatives), not measured search volume. Orgvue's own [workforce planning page](https://www.orgvue.com/solutions/strategic-workforce-planning/workforce-planning-software/) supports its scenario-planning scope. We do not claim its dashboard cannot answer our demonstration questions.
